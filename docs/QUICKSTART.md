# Your first workout

Open [Milo](https://gwilltsherman-byte.github.io/milovancroton/). Pick Muscle, Strength, Ladder, Conditioning, or a mix. With more than one, tap your main goal first: picks are numbered, and the first builds, the second gets a moderate dose and the rest keep what you have. Choose your training days and weight units, then build your week.

Milo starts with a full gym and 60 minutes per training day. Change equipment, priorities, weekdays, time and experience in Settings. Choose Ladder goals before expecting Ladder sessions; until you do, Today's main button opens the goal chooser.

## Today

Tap Start Day to open the workout directly. The daily check-in remains optional; skipped answers are not treated as poor recovery. An optional health check is available in Settings.

Together runs skills, heavy lifting, other Ladder work, Muscle, then conditioning. When a section finishes, continue to the next part or leave it for later.

## Log a set

The next-set screen shows one exercise and set. Check the suggested load and reps before tapping Done. Enter what you actually did when it differs.

RIR means reps left in the tank. RPE rates effort; a lifting RPE of 8 means roughly two reps remain. Conditioning asks for the effort of the whole activity instead.

Done starts rest. Use Skip rest or add 30 seconds as needed. List shows the whole workout and lets you edit sets. Its back arrow leaves the session open. An old unfinished session shows its start date.

Warm-ups stay separate from working sets. Ladder may ask for each side, a hold duration, or the assistance and load used. Keep form and setup accurate because they affect progression proof.

## Finish and review

Finish saves the workout. Unlogged sets are handled through the finish flow; they are not silently counted as completed work.

Progress combines weekly training, main lifts, goals and recovery. Filter by section for detail. Set an estimated-max, reps-at-load or weekly-muscle-sets goal. Projections need enough sessions and a clear upward trend.

At the start of a new week, Today can show a dismissible recap of the previous week. Your goals show their current progress.

## Conditioning

Start the planned timer after lifting. Keep Milo open for interval cues. Done opens the activity log; confirm actual minutes and effort before saving.

Use Log activity for a walk, sport or other outside session. Select the intensity you actually sustained. Vigorous minutes count twice toward the weekly target; light minutes remain in your log without guideline credit.

## Keep your training

In Settings, export a backup regularly. Choose encryption when the file needs a passphrase. Keep that passphrase somewhere safe. Restore the file on another device; devices do not sync.

On iPhone, use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen. Open that installed copy before recording workouts. Load once while connected for offline use. See [installation guidance](../RUNNING.md).
