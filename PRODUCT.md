# Milo van Croton

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent people training for muscle growth, strength and calisthenics. Inferred from the existing product: phones are used during workouts; larger screens support planning and reviewing progress.

## Product Purpose

Plan training, record real sets, resume sessions and understand progress. Success means the next action is clear, logging is quick, and training history remains safe.

## Operating Context

Muscle manages hypertrophy blocks, volume and recovery. Strength builds equipment-based plans and tracks working weights. Ladder maps goals to exercise progressions and readiness. Together combines their weeks and check-ins.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep all four trainers, existing exercise and research content, session editing, timers, settings, imports, exports and undo behavior. Preserve local storage keys, data schemas, hub integration, deep links and offline support. There are no accounts, analytics or cloud sync. Plain JavaScript, HTML templates and isolated shadow roots; static hosting with no build step. Keep security sanitization and the script integrity/CSP workflow.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Milo van Croton. The user requests a complete replacement visual design and delegates design decisions. Apple, Stripe and Linear are references for principles, not templates. No new product claims or fabricated workout history.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, docs/FEATURES.md, current app.js and index.html, existing local font assets and regression tests. Existing copy and data define functionality. Screenshots used for verification contain only local test actions.

## Product Principles

- Make the next training action obvious.
- Prefer readable numbers and comfortable controls over decoration.
- Explain estimates and progression criteria honestly.
- Protect user history and keep offline workflows complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, usable mobile targets and reduced-motion support are required by the brief. Retain light, dark and system theme options.
