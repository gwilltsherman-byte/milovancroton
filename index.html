Works offline after the first load. Your data stays in this browser; use Backup before moving devices or clearing browser data. Units and training preferences are in each trainer’s Settings.

Back up your data

One backup holds everything from Muscle, Strength and Ladder. Make one before you delete the app, then restore it after you add the app again.

Restore

Paste a backup, or pick a saved backup file. Whatever the backup holds replaces what the app has now; you can undo it afterwards.
